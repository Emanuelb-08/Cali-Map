Iconic Places

Edifications

The capital of Valle del Cauca offers various tourist spots to enjoy its architecture.

Simón Bolívar Monument

The Simón Bolívar monument is a pedestrian sculpture located in the Plazoleta of the Municipal Administrative Center (CAM).

La Ermita

La Ermita Church is a Catholic temple built near the Cali River, dedicated to the Lord of the Cane and Our Lady of the Conception.

Solidarity Monument

The Solidarity Monument is a sculpture inaugurated in 1995, symbolizing unity and collective spirit.

Parks

Experience the natural beauty and cultural heritage of Cali's iconic parks.

Plazoleta Jairo Varela

Jairo Varela Plaza is an urban space enriched with gastronomy and tribute to the city's music culture.

Cat Park

Cat Park's main attraction is the Tejada Cat, with cats throughout the park and urban art around the area.

Parque Artesanal Loma de La Cruz

At Parque Artesanal Loma de La Cruz, you can enjoy cultural shows weekly and artisan crafts.

Experiences

Locations

In Cali, you can find buildings that showcase the city's cultural and recreational attractions.

Salsa Museum

The Salsa Museum gathers and displays the musical culture and history of salsa both globally and in Cali.

Cali Zoo

At the Cali Zoo, you can explore trails full of nature and reconnect with biodiversity.

Caliwood Museum of Cinematography

Caliwood is the first Museum of Cinematography in Colombia's history, founded in 2008.

Viewpoints

In Cali, there are plazas where you can enjoy the natural beauty of the city from an iconic viewpoint.

Christ the King Monument

The Christ the King Monument was inaugurated in 1953 and is one of the city's most iconic landmarks.

Sebastián de Belalcázar Viewpoint

This viewpoint offers panoramic views of the city and a historic monument of Belalcázar.

Cerro de las Tres Cruces

The Hill of the Three Crosses is a landmark with three crosses and a broad city overlook.

Events

Holidays

In the capital of Valle del Cauca, artistic and cultural fairs take place where you can enrich your knowledge.

International Book Fair

The International Book Fair is held annually in Cali and brings together readers and authors from across the region.

Cali Fair

The Cali Fair is an annual event celebrating culture, music, and tradition in the city.

Cali International Film Fair

At the Cali International Film Fair, cultural and cinematic diversity is celebrated.

Festivals

In the capital of Valle del Cauca, cultural festivals are occasionally held where you can enjoy traditional activities and attractions.

Petronio Álvarez Festival

At the Petronio Álvarez Festival, a large number of artists gather to celebrate the region's musical heritage.

World Salsa Festival

The World Salsa Festival is celebrated annually in Cali, bringing dance and music to the city.

Cali International Theater Festival

The Cali International Theater Festival brings performing arts to different neighborhoods in the city.

Gastronomy

Cultural Dishes

In many places around the city, you can enjoy iconic dishes from Valle del Cauca.

Chuleta Valluna

Chuleta Valluna is a classic dish from Valle del Cauca served with rice, fried plantains, and lime.

Sancocho de Gallina

Sancocho de gallina is a typical Colombian dish made with chicken, plantain, and corn.

Arroz Atollado

Arroz Atollado combines pork, chicken, and sausage with rice and vegetables.

Traditional Sweets

In Cali, you can enjoy delicious traditional sweets.

Cholado

Cholado is a typical chilled dessert from Valle del Cauca made with shaved ice, fruits, and condensed milk.

Cocada

Cocadas are a sweet made with grated coconut, condensed milk, and sugar or panela.

Manjar Blanco

Manjar blanco is a sweet cream made with milk, sugar, and cornstarch or rice flour.
