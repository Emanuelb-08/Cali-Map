Iconic Places

Edifications

The capital of Valle del Cauca offers various tourist spots to enjoy its architecture.

Simón Bolívar Monument

The Simón Bolívar monument is a pedestrian sculpture located in the Plazoleta of the Municipal Administrative Center (CAM). It was inaugurated in 1922 as a tribute to the independence leader.

La Ermita

La Ermita Church is a Catholic temple built at the beginning of the 17th century near the Cali River, dedicated to the 'Lord of the Cane' and 'Our Lady of the Conception.'

Solidarity Monument

The Solidarity Monument is a sculpture inaugurated in 1995, symbolizing the collective spirit, unity, and solidarity of the people of Cali. It is made of steel and stands as a powerful visual landmark.

Parks

Experience the natural beauty and cultural heritage of Cali's iconic parks.

Plazoleta Jairo Varela

Jairo Varela Plaza is an urban space enriched with gastronomy, featuring a brass trumpet sculpture that forms the word 'Niche' in tribute to Jairo Varela and his group.

Cat Park

Cat Park's main attraction is the Tejada Cat, with cats scattered throughout the park. The urban art of different artists is displayed across the area, creating a vibrant nearby atmosphere.

Parque Artesanal Loma de La Cruz

At Parque Artesanal Loma de La Cruz, you can enjoy cultural shows weekly and purchase artisan wood crafts. It is also the place where the story of Cresencio de la Cruz is remembered and celebrated.

Experiences

Locations

In Cali, you can find buildings that showcase the city's cultural and recreational attractions.

Salsa Museum

The Salsa Museum is a place that gathers and displays musical culture and its history both worldwide and in Cali. It is also known as the oldest Salsa Museum in the world.

Cali Zoo

At the Cali Zoo, you can explore trails full of nature and reconnect with biodiversity. You can learn about animal species in immersive spaces designed for a unique experience.

Caliwood Museum of Cinematography

Caliwood is the first Museum of Cinematography in Colombia's history, founded in 2008. It is a cultural institution that exhibits items and artifacts related to film and audiovisual heritage.

Viewpoints

In Cali, there are plazas where you can enjoy the natural beauty of the city from an iconic and cultural viewpoint.

Monumento a Cristo Rey

The Christ the King Monument was inaugurated in 1953 to commemorate 50 years since the end of the Thousand Days' War. It is a 26-meter-tall statue on a 5-meter pedestal with a panoramic view over the city.

Sebastián de Belalcázar Viewpoint

This is a place where the statue of Belalcázar, inaugurated in 1937, stands alongside a viewpoint that offers a wide panoramic view of the city. Cultural and historical heritage converge here.

Cerro de las Tres Cruces

In this monument, built in 1937 and inaugurated in 1938, there are three crosses: the central one is 26 meters tall and 11 meters wide, and the side ones are 2 meters wide. It is a beloved site for panoramic photos and local history.

Events

Holidays

In the capital of Valle del Cauca, artistic and cultural fairs take place where you can enrich your knowledge.

International Book Fair

The International Book Fair of Cali is an event held annually from October 24 to November 3 at the Bulevar del Río, where readers and authors come together in a cultural experience.

Cali Fair

The Cali Fair is an annual event that takes place between December 25 and 30, featuring cultural and artistic rhythms performed by national and international artists.

Cali International Film Fair

At the Cali International Film Fair, cultural and cinematic diversity is celebrated, and meetings between creators and audiences take place.

Festivals

In the capital of Valle del Cauca, cultural festivals are occasionally held where you can enjoy traditional activities and attractions.

Petronio Álvarez Festival

At the Petronio Álvarez Festival, a large number of artists gather to play musical instruments inherited from the region's first settlers, bringing the history of the region to life.

World Salsa Festival

The World Salsa Festival is celebrated annually in Cali, where dance competitions and artistic performances take place, turning the city into a center of cultural energy.

Cali International Theater Festival

The Cali International Theater Festival (FITCali) is held annually in a decentralized way to reach the largest audience possible and bring them closer to theater and performance art.

Gastronomy

Cultural Dishes

In many places around the city, you can enjoy iconic dishes from Valle del Cauca.

Chuleta Valluna

Chuleta Valluna is a classic dish from Valle del Cauca that features a pork chop served with rice, fried plantains, and lime.

Sancocho de Gallina

Sancocho de gallina is a typical Colombian dish made with free-range chicken, plantain, and corn, usually served with rice, avocado, and chili.

Arroz Atollado

Arroz Atollado is a traditional dish from Valle del Cauca that combines pork, chicken, and sausage with rice, and includes potatoes, peas, and cilantro.

Traditional Sweets

In Cali, you can enjoy delicious traditional sweets.

Cholado

Cholado is a typical chilled dessert from Valle del Cauca made with shaved ice, fresh fruits, and condensed milk; it can be accompanied by grated coconut and additional toppings.

Cocada

Cocadas are a sweet made with grated coconut, condensed milk, and sugar or panela.

Manjar Blanco

Manjar blanco is a sweet cream made with milk or almond milk, sugar, and cornstarch or rice flour.
