Iconic Places

Edifications

The capital of Valle del Cauca offers various tourist spots to enjoy its architecture.

Simón Bolívar Monument

The Simón Bolívar monument is a pedestrian sculpture located in the Plazoleta of the Municipal Administrative Center (CAM). It was inaugurated in 1922 as a tribute to the 'Father of the Nation' at the site where the liberator gave his speech on his way south.

La Ermita

La Ermita Church is a Catholic temple that was built at the beginning of the 17th century near the Cali River, dedicated to the "Lord of the Cane" and "Our Lady of Solitude". In 1942, the church was remodeled into the form we can see today, inspired by the Ulm Main Church in Germany.

Solidarity Monument

The Solidarity Monument is a sculpture inaugurated in 1995, symbolizing the collective spirit, unity, and solidarity of the people of Cali. It is made of quartz and slate imported from Italy.

Parks

Experience the natural beauty and cultural heritage of Cali's iconic parks.

Plazoleta Jairo Varela

Jairo Varela Plaza is a place enriched with gastronomy, featuring a trumpet sculpture that forms the word "Niche" in tribute to Jairo Varela and his group Niche, inaugurated in 2010. On the trumpets, you can listen to and read about salsa as the cultural heritage of Cali.

Cat Park

Cat Park's main attraction is the Tejada Cat, with its cats scattered throughout the park, where the urban art of different artists is displayed, surrounded by the natural beauty of the flora.

Parque Artesanal Loma de La Cruz

At Parque Artesanal Loma de La Cruz, you can enjoy cultural shows weekly and purchase artisan wood crafts. It is also the place where the story of Cresencio and Juana is told, about their love under Afro-Caleña slavery in the 15th century.

Experiences

Locations

In Cali, you can find buildings that showcase the city's cultural and recreational attractions.

Salsa Museum

The Salsa Museum is a place that gathers and displays musical culture and its history both worldwide and in Cali. It is also known as the oldest Salsa Museum in the world, opened in 1968.

Cali Zoo

At the Cali Zoo, you can explore trails full of nature and reconnect with biodiversity. You can learn about animal species in immersive spaces designed for their well-being.

Caliwood Museum of Cinematography

Caliwood is the first Museum of Cinematography in Colombia's history, founded in 2008. It is a cultural institution that exhibits items and artifacts related to the art and culture of cinema.

Viewpoints

In Cali, there are plazas where you can enjoy the natural beauty of the city from an iconic and cultural viewpoint.

Monumento a Cristo Rey

The Christ the King Monument was inaugurated in 1953 to commemorate 50 years since the end of the Thousand Days' War. It is a 26-meter-tall statue on a 5-meter-high pedestal located on Cerro de los Cristales, where you have a view of the entire city.

Sebastián de Belalcázar Viewpoint

This is a place where the statue of Belalcázar, inaugurated in 1937, is located along with a viewpoint that offers a wide, panoramic view of the city. Cultural gatherings are also held here weekly on Sundays.

Cerro de las Tres Cruces

In this monument, built in 1937 and inaugurated in 1938, there are 3 crosses: the central one is 26 meters tall and 11 meters wide, and the side ones are 22 meters tall and 8 meters wide. There is also a spacious viewpoint of the city here.

Events

Holidays

In the capital of Valle del Cauca, artistic and cultural fairs take place where you can enrich your knowledge.

International Book Fair

The International Book Fair of Cali is an event that is held annually from October 24 to November 3 at the Bulevar del Río. Here, a cultural experience in literature is offered, with meetings with authors, book exhibitions, and educational workshops.

Cali Fair

The Cali Fair is an annual event that takes place between December 25 and 30, featuring cultural and artistic rhythms performed by national and international orchestras. It is one of the most important tourist events in Colombia.

Cali International Film Fair

At the Cali International Film Fair, cultural and cinematic diversity is celebrated, and meetings between creators and audiences take place.

Festivals

In the capital of Valle del Cauca, cultural festivals are occasionally held where you can enjoy traditional activities and attractions.

Petronio Álvarez Festival

At the Petronio Álvarez Festival, a large number of artists gather to play musical instruments inherited from the region's first settlers, bringing the history and tradition of the inherited culture to Cali.

World Salsa Festival

The World Salsa Festival is celebrated annually in Cali, where dance competitions and artistic performances take place, turning it into a center of cultural and social enrichment.

Cali International Theater Festival

The Cali International Theater Festival (FITCali) is held annually in a decentralized way to reach the largest audience possible and bring them closer to the world of theater. Works cover emotional themes, relationships, Afro identities, among others.

Gastronomy

Cultural Dishes

In many places around the city, you can enjoy iconic dishes from Valle del Cauca.

Chuleta Valluna

Chuleta Valluna is a classic dish from Valle del Cauca that features a pork chop served with rice, fried plantains, and lime.

Sancocho de Gallina

Sancocho de gallina is a typical Colombian dish made with free-range chicken, plantain, and corn, usually served with rice, avocado, and chili.

Arroz Atollado

Arroz Atollado is a traditional dish from Valle del Cauca that combines pork, chicken, and sausage with rice, also adding potatoes, peas, and cilantro.

Traditional Sweets

In Cali, you can enjoy delicious traditional sweets.

Cholado

Cholado is a typical chilled dessert from Valle del Cauca made with shaved ice, fresh fruits, and condensed milk; it can be accompanied by grated coconut and fruit syrup.

Cocada

Cocadas are a sweet made with grated coconut, condensed milk, and sugar or panela.

Manjar Blanco

Manjar blanco is a sweet cream made with milk or almond milk, sugar, and cornstarch or rice flour.
